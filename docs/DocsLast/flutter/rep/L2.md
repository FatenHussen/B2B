---

## Products — `FE-RP-CAT`

`GET /app/rep/products` · EP-RP-010 — union of SKUs from channels this rep belongs to. Every row **must** show `channel.id` + `channel.name` (opposite of retailer).

Filters: search, category, brand, `filter[channel_id]` if more than one channel, offer/available, optional barcode. Money + availability same as retailer (integers, enum, no stock qty).

Quote: `POST /app/pricing/quote` with a zone the rep **covers**. Body has no prices.

Offers: if shown, `company` stays null — do not copy channel name onto the offer card.

---

## Customers — `FE-RP-CUST`

`GET /app/rep/customers`. Add: `POST /app/rep/customers` with **two** keys:

1. `X-Idempotency-Key` per tap.  
2. `client_op_id` UUID for the local draft — persist until 201; **never rotate on retry**. Duplicate returns the existing shop.

Body: `shop_name`, `owner_name`, `phone`, `zone_id` (already covered), `activity_type_id`, optional GPS. No email. Status `pending_sync`. Offline: keep the same `client_op_id`.

---

## Extra zone — `FE-RP-ZONE`

`POST /app/rep/zones` `{ zone_id, note }` → `pending_approval`. Do **not** enable that zone in filters until later approval. 422 if the zone is outside **channel** coverage.

---

## Out of scope

`GET /app/rep/zones/{id}/shops` (L3), carts, submit, assignments, handover, wallet.
