# BE3-FIN02 — Payments with a unique receipt

## Requirements

1. Sources: rep app, retailer app, or direct accountant entry.
2. A payment is against a specific invoice or on account.
3. The receipt number is generated by one side and read by the other; two payments with the same number are impossible even across repeated sync (REQ-IN-01, BR-11).
4. Automatic allocation on account is oldest first, with manual reallocation available.

## Acceptance criteria

_Write one test per criterion. Name the test after the criterion._

- [ ] A duplicate receipt number is refused at the database level, not only in validation.
- [ ] A payment updates the retailer receivable, the rep wallet and the channel ledger in one transaction.
